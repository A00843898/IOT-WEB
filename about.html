BIOGRAPHY

David Tennant (born David John McDonald on April 18, 1971, in Bathgate, Scotland) is one of the most versatile and genuinely beloved actors of his generation. Growing up in Ralston, Renfrewshire, he fell in love with acting at a very young age inspired by the very stories that would later mark his career. Driven by that early passion, he trained at the prestigious Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama, quickly building a reputation for his magnetic stage presence and fierce commitment with the Royal Shakespeare Company.

After making an impression in standout British television projects like Casanova (2005) and bringing the unhinged Barty Crouch Jr to life in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, his career reached a whole new orbit. In 2005, he stepped into the TARDIS as the Tenth Doctor in Doctor Who. His run was nothing short of a cultural phenomenon, by blending high energy, quick witted charm, and heartbreaking vulnerability, he defined an entire era of the show and captured the hearts of fans all around the globe.

Off-screen, David’s life is just as full. In 2011, he married actress and producer Georgia Moffet, who he met in 2008 while filming the iconic Doctor Who episode "The Doctor's Daughter". Together, they share a vibrant family life with their five children, Ty, Olive, Wilfred, Doris, and Birdie.

FACTS ABOUT HIM

  • Vocal LGBTQ+ Ally: David consistently uses his platform to stand up for the LGBTQ+ community, frequently advocating for trans rights, wearing pride pin badges on national television, and talking equality, acceptance, and inclusion wherever he goes.
  • The Iconic Sheen-Antics: His role in Good Omens sparked an amazing real life friendship with Michael Sheen. Their effortless, chaotic chemistry led to their hit comedy series Staged, filmed during lockdown, which proved their banter is pure gold both on and off script.
  • A Musically Inspired Name: Because there was already a David McDonald registered in the actors' union, he chose the stage name "Tennant" inspired by Neil Tennant, the lead singer of the Pet Shop Boys. He later officially changed his legal name.
  • Forever Devoted to the Stage: Despite being a global screen star, theatre remains his grounding home. He regularly returns to the stage, earning rave reviews for powerhouse leads in Hamlet, Richard II, and Macbeth.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS

  • Doctor Who (2005–2010, 2022–2023): As the Tenth (and later Fourteenth) Doctor, David led one of the most iconic and universally beloved eras in television history, creating a legacy that cemented him as a fan-favorite Time Lord forever.
  • Broadchurch (2013–2017): He starred as the relentless DI Alec Hardy alongside Olivia Colman in this critically acclaimed crime drama, delivering a masterclass in subtlety and emotional depth.
  • Good Omens (2019–2026): As the swaggering yet endearingly complex demon Crowley, David forms an iconic duo with Michael Sheen’s Aziraphale. Their magnetic dynamic brings Neil Gaiman and Terry Pratchett’s universe to life, cementing the show as a global critically acclaimed hit.
  • Jessica Jones (2015–2019): Playing the terrifying mind-controlling villain Kilgrave (The Purple Man) in Marvel’s Netflix series, he gave us one of the most chilling, critically praised antagonist performances in modern TV.
  • Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire (2005): He left an unforgettable mark on the Wizarding World as the calculating and unhinged Death Eater Barty Crouch Jr.
  • Shakespearean Acclaim: A powerhouse on stage with the Royal Shakespeare Company, most notably starring in the groundbreaking 2008 production of Hamlet, which was later adapted for television.
